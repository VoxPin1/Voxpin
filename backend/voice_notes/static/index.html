VoxPin

Think it, Jot it, Do it

A voice pin for the ideas we cannot afford to lose.

We’re Rian, Anay, and Nihar. Together we’re building VoxPin: a small device that turns what we say into notes, translations, reminders, and help — without live tracking, and without storing audio clips.

We have been friends for many years, and we enjoy working together. That friendship is the reason this project exists. We already knew how we think, how we argue, and how we finish things — so when we sat down to build something of our own, we did not have to start from zero.

We look at the economy around us, and at everything people are rushing to build right now, and it is easy to feel like the world is loud with products that do not really matter. We wanted to do something meaningful together: something we would actually use and carry with us, that solves a real problem, and that we would still be proud of years from now.

That problem is simple. Thoughts disappear. A phrase we need in another language is gone before we can type it. A promise we made out loud never makes it onto a calendar. We built VoxPin so a hold of the pin is enough — think it, jot it, do it — and then the pin goes quiet again.

Our device

A voice pin we can carry, tap, and talk to.

VoxPin is a small device with a tiny screen. Keep it wherever it is useful and carry it with us. Hold to talk. The pin transcribes what we say and routes it — a note, a translation, a reminder, or a check-in with family — then it stops listening. We designed it so help is always one ask away, and never a background stream.

  • Take notes We say “take notes…” and the pin saves the words to our notes as text. The audio is not kept.
  • Translate this We speak a phrase, the pin answers in the language we chose, and the translation is saved with our notes so we can come back to it.
  • Remind me We set reminders by voice so they land on our calendar instead of getting lost in conversation.
  • Help, only when asked A tap can share where we are. A hold can ask for help. Nothing is streamed in the background, and location is never live-tracked.
