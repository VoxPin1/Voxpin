VoxPin is a small pin you hold and talk to. It can save notes, say words in another language, set reminders, and send an SOS message to your parents. It only listens when you ask. It does not keep the sound.

Our pin

Your ideas. Anywhere.

VoxPin is a Waveshare pin with a tiny screen. You can keep it with you. Hold it to talk. It writes down what you say and can turn it into a note, a translation, or a calendar invite. Hold it for SOS and it can send a help message to your parents. Then it stops listening.

  • Record notes & ideas Say take notes and the pin saves the words as text. It does not save the sound.
  • Translate as you speak Say a phrase. The pin says it in the language you picked.
  • Send invites to your calendar Say a reminder out loud and it goes on your calendar so you do not forget.
  • Send SOS to your parents Hold the pin to send a help message to your parents. It can share where you are.

Tech stack

How VoxPin works

A Waveshare pin, a Mac helper, and Google do the job.

The pin is a Waveshare ESP32 S3 board. You talk. The pin hears you. A helper on the Mac turns speech into words. Google saves the note or reminder. Then the pin can talk back.

  • 1 Waveshare pin

    ESP32 S3 board

    Waveshare makes the board we wear. It has a tiny color screen, a mic, a speaker, a battery, and buttons. The program on it is C++.

  • 2 The Mac helper

    Python on the laptop

    It reads what you said. Then it can save a note, set a reminder, translate, or SOS your parents.

  • 3 Google and GitHub

    Cloud and website

    Calendar keeps reminders. A Doc keeps notes. The public website lives on GitHub.

  1. You talk Hold the pin and say a note, a reminder, or SOS.
  2. WiFi carries it The pin sends the sound to the Mac on the same WiFi.
  3. The helper reads it Python turns speech into words and picks what to do.
  4. Google saves it Notes go in a Doc. Reminders go on Calendar, with the length you said.
  5. The pin talks back It can speak the answer, and buzz about 10 minutes before a reminder.
